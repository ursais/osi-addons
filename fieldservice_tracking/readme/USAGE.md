1. Open a **Field Service Worker** form.
2. On the **Field Service Tracking** tab, enable **Worker Allow Tracking** and review
   last-known latitude, longitude, accuracy, and location time.
3. Users in the **FSM Tracking Dispatcher** group can also set match radius, tracking
   interval, fence radius, and route stage.
4. Tracking history is stored on the `fsm.tracking` model for each worker and related
   field service order.
